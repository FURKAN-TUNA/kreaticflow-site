Privacy Policy

Cutaway: Engine Simulator · KreaticFlow · Last updated 11 November 2026

Cutaway: Engine Simulator is an Android app that shows cutaway views of four engine layouts (I4, V6, V8, V12) with a throttle control and synthesized engine sound. The simulation and the sound run on your device. There is no account, no sign-up and no server operated by KreaticFlow.

Data stored on your device

The current version saves no user settings or progress. When it runs in an automated test loop (Firebase Test Lab Game Loop), it writes performance measurement files (frame times, audio timing) to the app's own storage on that device; the app does not send these files anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes all of its local files.

Ads

The current test version does not contain an advertising SDK and does not show ads. Later versions are planned to show ads via Google AdMob. When they do, Google may process the advertising ID and device identifiers for ad delivery and frequency capping. Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland will be asked for consent through Google's User Messaging Platform. See the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. This page will be updated before such a version is released.

Engine and analytics

The app is built with the Unity engine. Unity may send anonymous device hardware statistics (for example device model, operating system version and graphics hardware) to Unity Technologies; see the Unity Privacy Policy. If usage analytics (for example Firebase Analytics by Google) are added in a later version, they will be described here before that version is released.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell data. Apart from the services named above, no data is shared with third parties.

Deletion and requests

Uninstalling the app deletes all locally stored data. You can reset your advertising ID in your device settings. For deletion or other requests, contact us by email.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children.

Engines shown

The engines in the app are generic layouts. They are not models of a specific manufacturer's product, and the app is not affiliated with any manufacturer.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with an updated date.
